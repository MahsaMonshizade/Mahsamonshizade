Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Indiana University Bloomington, with a minor in Bioinformatics.

I work at the intersection of **machine learning, generative AI, and computational biology**, with experience in:

- LLMs, RAG & evaluation
- Deep learning & interpretable ML
- Biomedical and microbiome ML
- Large-scale bioinformatics & single-cell workflows

## Selected Work

- **ChemBench / TroubleRAG** — Scientific RAG, retrieval, reranking, and LLM evaluation
- **MicroKPNN-MT** — Biology-informed neural networks for microbiome prediction
- **MicrobiomeCF** — Confounder-aware biomedical machine learning
- **Single-Cell Viral & Microbial Transcriptomics** — Scalable single-cell RNA-seq workflows for studying microbial and viral activity in the rumen
